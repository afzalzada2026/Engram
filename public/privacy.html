PRIVACY POLICY | EFFECTIVE MARCH 2026
Local by default.

You can play ENGRAM without an account. Cloud Sync is optional and is only activated when you choose to sign in with Google.

DATA USED WITHOUT CLOUD SYNC

Scores, XP, streaks, preferences, and training history are stored locally on your device. They are not transmitted to ENGRAM. Clearing the app's storage deletes this local data.

OPTIONAL FIREBASE CLOUD SYNC

If you choose Google Cloud Sync, Firebase Authentication processes your Google account identifier and may provide your display name, email address, and profile image. Cloud Firestore stores your game progress, including scores, XP, streak, best level, and leaderboard entries.

This information is used only to authenticate you and synchronize your private progress across your devices. ENGRAM does not use it for advertising, profiling, or medical assessment.

SHARING AND PROCESSORS

ENGRAM does not sell personal data. Google Firebase acts as the service provider for optional authentication and cloud storage. Google's processing is governed by its applicable Firebase and Google Cloud terms.

RETENTION AND DELETION

Your cloud data is retained until you delete your cloud account. In ENGRAM, open Sync / Backup, choose Delete Cloud Account, and verify your Google identity. The private Firestore save and Firebase Authentication account are then deleted. Your local device data remains unless you separately reset it in Settings.

DELETE ACCOUNT INSTRUCTIONS

SECURITY

Cloud data is encrypted in transit. Firestore rules restrict each save to its authenticated owner. No public profile or public leaderboard is created by Cloud Sync.

CHILDREN

ENGRAM does not knowingly collect information from children for advertising or profiling. Cloud Sync should only be used by someone permitted to use a Google account under the rules that apply in their country.

HEALTH DISCLAIMER

ENGRAM is a game, not a medical device or diagnostic tool. Performance in the game does not measure intelligence, general memory, or brain health. Consult a qualified clinician about memory concerns.

CONTACT

Privacy or deletion questions can be sent to the developer support email listed on ENGRAM's Google Play Store page. The developer must add that support email to the store listing before publication.
