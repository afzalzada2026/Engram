Delete your ENGRAM cloud account

This deletes the private Firestore save and Firebase Authentication account associated with ENGRAM.

  1. Open the account manager below.
  2. Sign in with the Google account you used for Cloud Sync.
  3. Select Delete Cloud Account and confirm.
  4. Verify your Google identity when prompted.
OPEN ACCOUNT MANAGER

Deletion does not erase progress stored only on your current device. To remove that too, open Settings, Privacy, then Reset All Local Data.
